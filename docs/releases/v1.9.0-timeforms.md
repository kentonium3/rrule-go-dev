# v1.9.0: DTSTART and UNTIL forms

Parsed rules now retain the distinction between DATE, floating DATE-TIME, UTC,
and DATE-TIME with TZID. Floating DTSTART/UNTIL round-trip without `Z` or `TZID`,
including when an InLocation parser supplies an evaluation location. DATE starts
serialize canonically as `DTSTART;VALUE=DATE:YYYYMMDD` and their UNTIL stays DATE.
Legacy bare DATE starts remain accepted. Explicit `VALUE=DATE-TIME` is accepted
with or without TZID as appropriate. An explicit gap start preserves its original
nominal wall time in text, so serialization and reparsing retain its civil cadence.
An explicit floating UNTIL in a clock gap uses the offset before the gap, as RFC5545
§3.3.5 requires; the resulting boundary remains inclusive.

The parsers now reject these time-form combinations with the relevant part named:

- UTC or TZID DTSTART with a floating UNTIL.
- Floating DTSTART with a UTC UNTIL.
- DATE DTSTART with any DATE-TIME UNTIL, or any DATE-TIME DTSTART with DATE UNTIL.
- TZID applied to a UTC (`Z`) value or a DATE value, including canonical DATE syntax.
- A DTSTART VALUE parameter whose DATE/DATE-TIME declaration disagrees with its value.
- An inline DTSTART that conflicts with a separate/shared DTSTART's form or anchor.

A TZID DTSTART with a UTC `Z` UNTIL remains accepted and retains its output.
The constraints are checked after collecting both parts, independent of inline
attribute order, two-line order, or shared DTSTART placement in a set. Every
RRULE and legacy EXRULE undergoes the time-form check before last-wins or ignored
behavior. EXRULE remains ignored for evaluation; unrelated EXRULE properties stay
ignored. Multiple RRULE lines retain their historical last-wins behavior.

An RRULE fragment without DTSTART retains its historical InLocation parsing and
UTC-with-Z UNTIL output, including floating and DATE UNTIL input. Programmatic
`NewRRule` options retain UTC-with-Z UNTIL output in every ordinary location.
Copied parsed options safely share immutable private descriptors. Replacing a
public DTSTART/UNTIL value invalidates only that part's descriptor; time-only
public setters deliberately replace that part's parsed semantics. Internal set
transfers preserve descriptors and the exact caller-supplied location pointer.
A detached `StrToDtStart` time value carries its instant, without a separate
lexical-form transport guarantee.

This minor release contains a deliberately approved source compatibility change:
`ROption` now has a private field. Existing exported signatures and keyed literals
remain valid, but external positional literals no longer compile. Migrate them to
keyed literals, for example `rrule.ROption{Freq: rrule.DAILY, Count: 3}`.
No new exported identifier or dependency is added; Go1.16 remains supported.

The TZID-on-UTC rejection is based on an independently verified idea from
teambition/rrule-go PR68. Its suffix-equality pair check and local UNTIL output
were not adopted: RFC5545 requires UTC UNTIL for a TZID start. MIT attribution
and the modified-copy README notice are retained. This note is private release
preparation; it does not authorize publication, a tag, or a release.
